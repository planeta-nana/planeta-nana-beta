october 16

Today went into a community garden and I found a place to hide so I could hit my vape but then I guess they closed up while I was hiding because when I tried to get out the big iron gate was locked up and it dawned on me that I was locked in this very well fenced garden with no idea who has the keys or when they’re coming back. Blah blah blah

I ended up climbing a ladder onto the roof of their shed and then scaling a 12 foot tall chain link fence into the back corner of another (open) park that smelled like human shit but at least I made it out ok.
